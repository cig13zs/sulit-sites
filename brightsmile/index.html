Tanong ng mga pasyente

Do you accept HMO?
Yes for selected providers. Bring your HMO card and a valid ID, we confirm coverage at the front desk before any procedure.
Masakit po ba?
We use proper local anesthesia for extractions and fillings, and we wait for it to take full effect. Tell us anytime if you feel something, we stop and add more.
Can I walk in without an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome but booked patients go first. Same-day slots are usually open on weekdays before noon.
Pwede bang installment ang braces?
Yes. Typical arrangement is a down payment then monthly payments across the treatment. Exact terms are given during the free consultation.
