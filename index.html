Questions owners ask

Sagot muna bago bayad.

Wala kaming photos o content. Kaya ba?
Yes. I write the text for you and can source clean, professional-looking photos if needed. Your job is just to confirm facts: prices, hours, address.
Why so much cheaper than agencies?
Agencies bill for account managers, offices, and sales teams. You pay me directly, one person, no middlemen. The site quality is the same because I use modern tools and templates refined across builds.
Ano mangyayari pagkatapos mo i-build?
You own the domain and accounts. If you take the Care Plan I handle updates and upkeep; if not, I hand over everything and teach you the basics. Either way, hindi ka nakakulong sa akin.
Pwede bang hati ang bayad?
Standard is 50% when we start revisions, 50% at launch. For larger builds (multi-page + booking), we can split into three.
Do you also do Facebook pages, Google Maps, chatbots?
Yes. Google Business Profile setup comes with Business Site. Messenger ordering bots for shops and carinderias are an add-on, ask for a demo.
